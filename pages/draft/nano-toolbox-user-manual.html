--- layout: null title: NANO toolbox User Manual permalink: /_draft/nano-toolbox/ sitemap: false search: false --- {% capture manual_markdown %} # NANO toolbox User Manual **Version 1.1.0 · Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)** Use NANO toolbox to see your receiver’s position, apply RTK corrections, and record data. ## 1. Connect your receiver 1. [Download NANO toolbox v1.1.0](https://github.com/datagnss/app-releases/releases/download/nano-toolbox-v1.1.0/NANO-toolbox-v1.1.0-windows-amd64.zip). Extract the ZIP and double-click `NANO-toolbox.exe`. 2. Power on the receiver and connect it to the computer. Under **Connection**, select the receiver’s COM port in **Device**. Set **Baud rate** to match your receiver. The toolbox starts at **230400**. 3. Click **Connect**. Look for live data in **Position**. If the receiver is still looking for satellites, it may take a moment to show a position. ![NANO toolbox connected on COM47 at 230400 baud, with live position status]({{ '/images/gnss/nano-toolbox/connection-position.png' | relative_url }}) ## 2. Set up RTK corrections With the receiver connected over serial, open **Corrections**. - For **NTRIP**, choose `ntripcli`. Enter the server address, port, mountpoint, and any login details from your provider. - If your provider gives you a **TCP** address instead, choose `tcpcli` and enter the address and port. Click **Connect** on the **Corrections** page. If your provider asks for your rover position, turn on **Send GGA to caster (every 5 seconds)**. A rising frame count means corrections are coming in. Check **Position** for **RTK Fixed**. ## 3. Change receiver settings You can skip this section if your receiver is already working as you need. - Open **Messages** to choose which messages the receiver sends. Click **Set** to apply them. Select **Save config to flash** if you want to keep the settings after restart. - Open **Config** to change how often the receiver sends its position. Under **Output rate**, click **Poll** to read the current setting, then choose **1 Hz**, **5 Hz**, or **10 Hz** and click **Set**. Use **Save to flash** to keep the change. ## 4. Record data or open a plot 1. Before connecting, open **Log** and turn on **Log to file**. Turn on **Log base stream to file** if you also want to save the incoming corrections. 2. Connect the receiver and, if needed, the correction source. Click **Disconnect** when you’re done to finish saving the files. 3. Open the `logs/` folder beside the toolbox. Keep each `.dat` file with its matching `.dat.tag` file. For a live plot, put [RTKPLOT and its required files](https://github.com/tomojitakasu/RTKLIB_bin/tree/rtklib_2.4.3) beside `NANO-toolbox.exe`, connect the receiver, and click **Plot** in **Position**. ## 5. Update NANO toolbox Disconnect the receiver, then open **About** and click **Check for Updates**. If an update is available, follow the prompts and check **About** again after the app restarts. If your copy has no updater, close the toolbox and replace just the executable with the one from the [latest v1.1.0 ZIP](https://github.com/datagnss/app-releases/releases/tag/nano-toolbox-v1.1.0). Keep your existing settings and logs. ## 6. Troubleshooting - **No COM port:** Check the receiver’s power and data cable. Install the USB-to-serial driver if Windows needs it. - **The COM port won’t open:** Close any other app using that port, then try again. - **Connected but no position:** Check the baud rate and that the receiver sends GGA on that port. Give the antenna a clear view of the sky. - **No corrections or no RTK Fixed:** Check the server details and mountpoint. If corrections are coming in, make sure the receiver accepts them on the connected port and allow time for a fix. If you contact [DATAGNSS](https://www.datagnss.com), include the toolbox version, receiver model, and any error shown. Don’t share `NANO-toolbox.json`: it can contain your NTRIP password. {% endcapture %} {{ page.title | escape }} — Review draft
